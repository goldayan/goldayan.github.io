I use open source android application, [[https://antennapod.org/][antennapod]] to listen to podcast.
- [[https://syntax.fm/][Syntax.fm]] podcast is one of favorite, easy to listen and keeps me
updated on latest tech and ai news. 
- [[https://www.dwarkesh.com/podcast][dwarkesh]] podcast, in depeth ai tech episode

Blogs i will checkout regularly via RSS (Rich Site Summary)
- Sebastian Raschka
  - https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/
  - He is the author of LLM from scratch and Reasoning from scratch
  - His [[https://sebastianraschka.com/books/ml-q-and-ai/#table-of-contents][Machine learning Q and AI]] is free to read on web, check it out it has few nuggets
- Simon Willison 
  - https://simonwillison.net/entries/
  - Author of datasette and lot of small tools
  - He will post update on latest ai model, repost tech quotes from twitter and other source
- Hacker news
  - https://news.ycombinator.com/
  - Check it once or twice a day you will get to now most of important stuff happening around technology.

Sub reddit i follow via RSS and sometimes browse in web
- LocalLLaMA
  - https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/
  - New model release especially more discussion on local model and configuration.
- Pi Coding agent
  - https://www.reddit.com/r/PiCodingAgent/
  - Pi is minimal ai agent which is easy to customize so if something
    in ai is good, there will be plugin for it in pi.
